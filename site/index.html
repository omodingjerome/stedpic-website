The Real Risks of Buying Land in Uganda from Abroad

Abafele are real. And they target the Diaspora.

Fake or Disputed Titles

A seller shows you a seemingly valid title — but it's forged, already sold to someone else, or has an existing caveat you don't know about.

Wrong Size, Wrong Location

You pay for 100 by 100 ft. You get 89 by 100. Or the land shown on the title is in a completely different location than what you were shown.

Encroachment & Land Grabbing

Neighbors slowly shift beacons. Developers build on your land. Relatives occupy your plot. You only find out years later — if at all.

No Eyes on the Ground

You've invested your life savings from abroad but have no way to check on your property regularly, verify its condition, or know if something has changed.

StedPic exists to protect you from every one of these. Don't send money before we send you the truth.
